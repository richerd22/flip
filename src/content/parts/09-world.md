## Sampling around the world

**The short version:** Every scene samples its own way. Boom bap chopped drums off vinyl, the West Coast hired players to replay funk, French producers ran disco through a filter, jungle built a genre on a few seconds of a 1969 drum solo, and a 1989 Jamaican riddim sits under most of reggaeton and was still before a US federal court in September 2026. Three questions follow the practice everywhere: who is credited, who is paid, and whether a rhythm can be owned at all.

### How does hip-hop sample, style by style?

| Style | Where and when | What it does with a sample | Records to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boom bap | Mid-1980s on | Drum hits and loops off vinyl, swung on an SP-1200 or MPC | MC Shan, "The Bridge" (1986); KRS-One, "Return of the Boom Bap" (1993)[^now-wp-boombap][^now-wp-hiphop-production] |
| G-funk | US West Coast, early 1990s | 1970s funk grooves replayed by live musicians | Dr. Dre, "The Chronic" (1992); Snoop Dogg, "Doggystyle" (1993); Warren G, "Regulate... G Funk Era" (1994)[^now-wp-gfunk] |
| Chipmunk soul | Popularized in the 2000s | Soul vocals sped up until they squeak | Kanye West, "The College Dropout"[^now-wp-chipmunk] |
| Chopped and screwed | Houston | Whole records slowed down, with skipped beats and scratching | DJ Screw's tapes[^now-wp-screwed] |
| Sample drill | New York, 2021 on | A recognizable R&B or pop hook over drill drums | B-Lovee, "My Everything"[^now-complex-drill-2022] |

**Boom bap** is named for its own drums. The phrase goes back to T La Rock's "It's Yours" (1984), where it is an ad-lib imitating kick and snare, and KRS-One's 1993 album "Return of the Boom Bap" fixed it to the sound of DJ Premier and Pete Rock.[^gfb-rbma-boombap-2013][^now-wp-boombap] The working method underneath is Marley Marl's: single kick, snare and hi-hat hits lifted off a record and reprogrammed, as on MC Shan's "The Bridge" in 1986.[^gfb-rtb-ubb-2021][^now-wp-hiphop-production]

**G-funk** went the other way. Dr. Dre's habit was to have musicians replay the parts he wanted, so a G-funk track may carry little or no direct sampling.[^now-wp-gfunk] A replay is a new recording, so under US law the request goes to the song's owners alone.[^now-payforplay-ch40] Oliver Keens argued in the Guardian in 2022 that this leaves an ethical question open, since the owner of the original recording is not paid.[^gfb-guardian-keens-2022][^now-wp-sampling] Dre usually gets credit for the sound; Cold 187um of Above the Law says he came up with it, name included, in 1991.[^gfb-complex-gfunk-2017][^now-wp-gfunk]

**Chipmunk soul**, named for Alvin and the Chipmunks, goes back at least to RZA in the mid-1990s; Just Blaze and the Heatmakerz worked it in the years Kanye West made it his calling card.[^gfb-rbma-chipmunk-2018][^now-wp-chipmunk] Trap, whose bass is an 808 kick tuned with a long decay to play notes, moved the sampling upstream.[^now-wp-trap] Producers sell "type beats" in a named artist's style, and "sample producers" write loop kits for other producers to build on; Frank Dukes' Kingsway Music Library has fed records by Drake, Kanye West, Kendrick Lamar and J. Cole.[^gfb-kingsway-about][^now-wp-hiphop-production][^now-wp-samplepack] Sabrina Carpenter's 2024 single "Espresso" is built on loops from a Splice pack called "Oliver: Power Tools Sample Pack III".[^gfb-musicradar-espresso-2024][^now-wp-splice]

### What is sample drill?

Around 2021 New York drill producers started dropping whole, familiar hooks over the genre's sliding 808s. Complex named it **sample drill** in May 2022 and called it a "nostalgic-yet-modern sound".[^now-complex-drill-2022] The marker record is B-Lovee's "My Everything," produced by Cash Cobain around Mary J. Blige's "Everything"; Complex counted 468,000 TikTok videos using it in 2022.[^now-complex-drill-2022]

Cash Cobain was plain about the paperwork. "My Everything" was cleared, he told Complex, and plenty else was not: "The ones that are just on YouTube, they not cleared."[^now-complex-drill-2022] He also drew a line at Fivio Foreign's album "B.I.B.L.E.," which samples Destiny's Child's "Say My Name." Too polished to count, in his view.[^now-complex-drill-2022]

### What is Jersey club?

Jersey club came out of Newark around the turn of the 2000s as a faster cousin of Baltimore club. DJ Tameil, one of its founders, dates it to 1999-2000 and says it was first called Brick City club; the name changed once producers from outside Newark took it up.[^gfb-fader-jersey-2014][^now-wp-jersey-club] The sound is chopped vocal samples over a triplet kick pattern and 808 bass, with breakbeats from Lyn Collins' "Think (About It)".[^now-wp-jersey-club] Its best-known sample is furniture: the squeaking bed from Trillville's "Some Cut".[^gfb-ringer-club-2022] Drake borrowed the style for his 2022 album "Honestly, Nevermind," and Lil Uzi Vert's "Just Wanna Rock" followed the same year.[^gfb-ringer-club-2022][^now-wp-jersey-club]

### How do house and French touch use samples?

House began as an edit. Frankie Knuckles spliced tape to stretch records that were too short for his floor, and the tracks that followed were built on loops of disco, soul and jazz-funk over Roland drum machines.[^now-wp-house] One of those machines is part sampler itself. The TR-909 is analog except for its crash, ride and hi-hats, which are samples of cymbals that Roland engineer Atsushi Hoshiai brought in from his own collection.[^gfb-attack-hoshiai-2024][^now-wp-909]

**French touch**, also called filter house, is the 1990s French version: a sample from a late-1970s or early-1980s disco record, looped, with a filter or phaser sweeping across it so the loop seems to open and close. The names are Motorbass, whose 1996 album "Pansoul" is largely samples and filtered loops, Daft Punk's "Homework" (1997), Stardust, Cassius, and Thomas Bangalter's Roulé label.[^now-wp-french-house]

Stardust's one single, "Music Sounds Better With You" (1998), is the method at its barest. The hook is a guitar phrase from Chaka Khan's "Fate" (1981), looped under a new vocal by Benjamin Diamond.[^gfb-routenote-stardust-2026]

### Why does jungle run on the Amen break?

Jungle producers took the Amen break, a few seconds of drumming from The Winstons' 1969 B-side "Amen, Brother," cut it apart and laid it over basslines and vocal samples from dub, reggae and dancehall.[^now-npr-amen-2017][^now-wp-amen][^now-wp-jungle] The break's own story is at [/breaks/](/breaks/). The drum and bass producer Roni Size told NPR: "It was just one of those breaks where you can just manipulate it."[^now-npr-amen-2017]

The genre's name may be a sample too. In Simon Reynolds' book "Energy Flash," MC Navigator traces the word to a chant that Rebel MC lifted from a Jamaican sound-system "yard-tape".[^now-wp-jungle] Drum and bass kept the same short list of breaks - Amen, Apache, Funky Drummer, "Think (About It)" - worked on Akai samplers sequenced from an Atari ST.[^gfb-reverb-jungle][^now-wp-dnb]

### What are big beat and trip-hop?

**Big beat** stacked heavy breakbeats, acid-house synth lines, loops from 1960s and 1970s funk and rock, and scraps of film and TV dialogue. The name has two origin stories: a 1989 coinage by Iain Williams of the band Big Bang, and Brighton's Big Beat Boutique club night, tied to Norman Cook and Skint Records.[^now-wp-bigbeat] Cook, as Fatboy Slim, made "The Rockafeller Skank" (1998) out of a Lord Finesse vocal, Just Brothers' "Sliced Tomatoes," a John Barry cue and more.[^now-wp-skank] He came away with none of the royalties. By his account four sample owners "all wanted 40%, or 50%," and once the shares were settled "there was none left for me."[^gfb-musicradar-skank-2025]

**Trip-hop** got its name from Mixmag's Andy Pemberton in June 1994, writing about DJ Shadow's "In/Flux" and other Mo' Wax records.[^gfb-mixmag-genres-2016][^now-wp-triphop] Portishead's "Dummy," released later that year, sounds like a stack of old soundtrack LPs. Much of it is the band. They recorded their own music, had it pressed to vinyl, wore the grooves down and sampled the pressings.[^now-pri-dummy-2019] The album also credits samples of other people, including Lalo Schifrin's "The Danube Incident" on "Sour Times" and Isaac Hayes' "Ike's Rap II" on "Glory Box".[^now-wp-dummy] Geoff Barrow never took to the name: "When I first heard somebody call us trip-hop, I hated it."[^gfb-musicradar-barrow-2025]

### What are plunderphonics and mashups?

**Plunderphonics** is John Oswald's word, from a 1985 essay, for music made openly out of other recordings. He gave his 1989 CD away free; the Canadian Recording Industry Association sent a cease-and-desist letter within a month, and under the settlement he handed over his remaining copies to be destroyed.[^gfb-vice-oswald-2016][^now-wp-plunderphonics]

The pop landmark is The Avalanches' "Since I Left You," released in Australia in November 2000.[^now-wp-sily] The usual count is more than 3,500 samples;[^gfb-sos-avalanches-2016] Wikipedia lists estimates nearer 900.[^now-wp-sily] The band made the record largely for themselves and kept no list of sources, so nobody can settle it.[^gfb-mixdown-sily-2020] The UK and North American release slipped to 2001 and went out without the samples nobody would clear, among them dialogue from the film "Midnight Run".[^gfb-mixdown-sily-2020]

Negativland's 1991 EP "U2," on SST, sampled U2's "I Still Haven't Found What I'm Looking For" along with a bootleg tape of radio host Casey Kasem's studio outtakes. Island Records sued, claiming the music and the artwork infringed copyright and would confuse buyers waiting for "Achtung Baby," and the EP was withdrawn.[^gfb-kqed-negativland-2014][^gfb-farout-negativland][^now-wp-u2ep]

A **mashup** lays one song's vocal over another's instrumental. Early markers are The Evolution Control Committee's "Whipped Cream Mixes" (1994), which set Public Enemy a cappellas over Herb Alpert, and Freelance Hellraiser's "A Stroke of Genie-us," which put Christina Aguilera over the Strokes at the start of the 2000s.[^gfb-treble-mashups][^now-wp-mashup]

### Why do vaporwave and lo-fi sound like old tapes?

Vaporwave, an internet genre of the early 2010s, is made almost entirely of slowed-down samples of 1980s and 1990s smooth jazz, lounge, R&B and elevator music under heavy reverb. Its founding documents are Daniel Lopatin's "Chuck Person's Eccojams Vol. 1" (2010), James Ferraro's "Far Side Virtual" (2011) and Macintosh Plus's "Floral Shoppe" (2011).[^gfb-perfectcircuit-vaporwave-2024][^now-wp-vaporwave]

Lo-fi hip-hop traces to the 2000s beat scene and Roland's SP-303 and SP-404, each of which has a lo-fi effect on the front panel. Nujabes and J Dilla are the names most often attached to it, and YouTube music streams carried it to a mass audience in the late 2010s.[^now-wp-lofi]

### Who owns the dembow riddim?

Reggaeton's beat is usually traced to Jamaica. Producers Steely & Clevie cut the "Fish Market" riddim in 1989 and reused its drum pattern on Shabba Ranks' "Dem Bow," which gave the rhythm its name.[^now-cmu-dembow-jul3][^now-wp-reggaeton] A **riddim** is a backing track made to be reused by many singers.

In 2021 Cleveland "Clevie" Browne and the heirs of Wycliffe "Steely" Johnson filed Browne v. Clark in a California federal court against more than 150 artists, Bad Bunny, Karol G and Daddy Yankee among them, and the three major labels, over a song list the press puts at about 1,800 to nearly 2,000.[^now-dmn-dembow-2026][^now-cmu-dembow-jul3][^gfb-rollingstone-ca-dembow-2026] On September 1, 2026 the judge gave the defendants summary judgment on the plaintiffs' selection-and-arrangement theory, which the defendants had called a "Frankenstein copyright" assembled from parts of three songs, without deciding whether the rhythm is original; the claims over songs alleged to sample Steely & Clevie's recordings directly go on.[^now-cmu-dembow-jul16][^fcl-browne-order-2026][^now-remezcla-2026] No appeal had been reported by September 4, 2026, and no court has found any named artist liable; the rulings are laid out at [/law/](/law/).[^now-jamaicaobserver-2026]

### What does Brazilian funk sample?

Funk carioca grew in Rio's favelas in the 1980s from Miami bass and freestyle records that DJs bought in Miami.[^now-wp-funk-carioca] Its first rhythmic base was a loop from Los Angeles, the Volt Mix, off a 1988 DJ Battery Brain single; it is in [the crate](/crate/).[^gfb-billboardbr-volt-2026][^now-wp-funk-carioca] The melodies are sampled too: horn stabs, accordion stabs, the opening of the "Rocky" theme.[^now-wp-funk-carioca] Since 2023 the car-audio strain, funk automotivo, has traveled abroad under the name "Brazilian phonk," a label not widely used in Brazil.[^now-wp-funk-carioca]

### How do Afrobeats and amapiano use samples?

Burna Boy's "Last Last" (2022) is built on Toni Braxton's "He Wasn't Man Enough," and Burna Boy said in 2022 that Braxton takes about 60% of the royalties. Producer Chopstix said Braxton's team was approached after the song was finished.[^now-okayafrica-2022]

Nigerian artists have since turned to their own back catalog. In 2024 Ayra Starr's "Jazzy's Song" drew on Wande Coal's "You Bad," Tems' "Love Me Jeje" on a 1999 original, and ID Cabasa remade Styl Plus's 2003 "Olufunmi."[^now-africanarguments-2024] Journalist Joey Akan sees the upside: "These [sample] records give an opportunity to the old folks to get new money." Publicist Gracey Mae sees what never comes out, because the original artist cannot be reached or asks too much.[^now-africanarguments-2024]

South Africa's amapiano has no single founder and a disputed birthplace within Gauteng. Its signature is the "log drum" bass sound, usually credited to the producer MDU aka TRP,[^gfb-mixmag-logdrum-2023] and its sample packs often include sounds taken from gqom.[^now-wp-amapiano]

### Why is K-pop full of old melodies?

Le Sserafim's 2026 single "Boompala" is built on Los del Rio's "Macarena," with both original writers credited. The Korea Herald counted it as part of a run: NCT Wish's "Ode to Love" draws on The Cranberries' "Ode to My Family," and Ive's "Attitude" uses the melody of Suzanne Vega's "Tom's Diner".[^now-koreaherald-2026] Classical music serves the same purpose: Blackpink's "Shut Down" (2022) rides Paganini's "La Campanella," and Red Velvet's "Feel My Rhythm" uses Bach's "Air on the G String".[^now-koreaherald-2026] Critic Lim Hee-yun says a long-lived melody helps K-pop reach non-fans; culture critic Kim Heon-sik warns of "relying on proven hits rather than presenting truly original creations".[^now-koreaherald-2026]

### Which Western hits sample Bollywood?

Truth Hurts' "Addictive" (2002), produced by DJ Quik, runs about 35 seconds of "Thoda Resham Lagta Hai," sung by Lata Mangeshkar for the 1981 film "Jyoti," and drew a lawsuit from composer Bappi Lahiri and the label Saregama.[^now-marshall-2012] The scholars who documented the case, Wayne Marshall and Jayson Beaster-Jones, argue such reuse "unsettles easy charges of appropriation" while still trading on exoticism. They set it beside Timbaland's beat for Jay-Z's "Big Pimpin'," built on the Egyptian song "Khusara".[^now-marshall-2012]

Dance music has dug in the same catalog: Four Tet's "Morningside" samples Lata Mangeshkar, Madlib's "More Rice" samples Kishore Kumar, and The Prodigy took Asha Bhosle from the film "Don".[^now-mixmag-2023]

### What gets sampled besides records?

Anything that makes a sound. DJ Shadow's "Endtroducing....." (1996), made on an Akai MPC60 with a Technics turntable and an Alesis ADAT tape recorder,[^gfb-djmag-endtroducing] samples John Carpenter's film "Prince of Darkness" and dialogue from "Twin Peaks".[^now-wp-endtroducing] James Ferraro's "Far Side Virtual" is punctuated by Windows system sounds and written to mimic ringtones.[^gfb-quietus-ferraro][^now-wp-vaporwave] The habit is older than the sampler. Pierre Schaeffer assembled his 1948 "Étude aux chemins de fer" from recordings of trains.[^now-wp-soundcollage]

Drum machines became sample sources themselves. Roland built the TR-808 from 1980 to 1983, and recordings of its sounds are now standard issue in music software.[^now-wp-808]

### Why is there a sped-up version of every song?

Miguel's "Sure Thing" peaked at No. 36 on the Hot 100 in 2010. In April 2023, after TikTok users pushed it from 81 to 105 BPM, it reached No. 15.[^now-ringer-2023] Labels noticed. Summer Walker and SZA got official sped-up releases in 2022 and 2023, and Cults made their own sped-up "Gilded Lily," about 10% faster.[^now-ringer-2023] LVRN's Amber Grimes: "It's just too late in the game to be questioning Gen Z or questioning TikTok."[^now-ringer-2023]

Billboard later found the practice industrialized. Universal runs a "Speed Radio" account that posts only sped-up versions of its releases, and Audiomack said label-made altered uploads rose from under 1,000 a quarter to about 6,000, starting at the end of 2022. A remix issued under the artist's own name counts toward that artist's chart position; one on an alter-ego account does not.[^now-billboard-spedup]

The slow version came first. DJ Screw, who died in 2000, built Houston's chopped and screwed style by slowing existing records to a crawl,[^gfb-houstonia-screw-2020] and "slowed + reverb" surfaced on YouTube years later, with a Houston producer called slater! among its uploaders.[^now-wp-screwed] When a 2020 TikTok credited the sound to slater!, Houston took offense and one headline called the style "gentrified" chopped and screwed. The producer offered $3,000 to have the video taken down and said Screw was his influence.[^gfb-wzak-screw-2020]

### Does a sample send listeners back to the original?

After Jack Harlow's "First Class" arrived on April 8, 2022, on-demand streams of Fergie's "Glamorous" went from 3,225,900 in the weeks before to 5,474,100 in the weeks after, a 70% rise by Luminate's count.[^now-variety-2022] It does not always work. Chartmetric, in case studies with no control group, found no lift for Rick Astley after Yung Gravy's "Betty (Get Money)."[^now-chartmetric-2022] By the count of Tracklib, which sells licensed samples, 17% of 2022's Hot 100 hits sampled an earlier song.[^now-tracklib-2022]

WhoSampled's counts are whatever its users have submitted to date. NPR put the Amen break in "at least 2,000 songs" in 2017; WhoSampled listed it 6,873 times in July 2025.[^now-npr-amen-2017][^gfb-routenote-most-sampled-2025]
