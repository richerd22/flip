# The Flip

In 1969 a soul band called The Winstons needed a B-side. They cut an instrumental, and about halfway through the drummer, Gregory Coleman, played four bars on his own.[^brk-musicradar-breaks-2022] Those few seconds have since been lifted into thousands of other records - WhoSampled listed 6,873 as of July 2025 - and they run through jungle and drum and bass.[^brk-routenote-2025] Coleman never saw a royalty for it.[^brk-mixmag-2015-11]

That is sampling in one story: a piece of an old recording put to work in a new one, a machine that made it possible, a genre built on the result, and a long argument about who should be paid. A flip is the trade's word for doing it well - taking a sound and turning it into something its makers never planned.
