The most sampled breaks, and who played them

The crate

21 breaks and sources everyone uses

The breakWhat gets sampledHeard inDid the players get paid?
Amen break
The Winstons - "Amen, Brother", 1969		A four-bar drum solo, about six seconds long. Played by Gregory "G.C." Coleman. sourceSalt-N-Pepa, N.W.A, Mantronix, The ProdigyNeither Coleman nor copyright owner Richard Spencer received royalties; a 2015 fundraiser gave Spencer a goodwill payment.
Funky Drummer
James Brown - "Funky Drummer", 1970		An eight-bar drum solo of about 20 seconds. Played by Clyde Stubblefield. sourcePublic Enemy, LL Cool J, George Michael, Ed SheeranStubblefield was not credited as a writer and saw little in royalties.
Think break
Lyn Collins - "Think (About It)", 1972		The drum loop, the tambourine and the shouted vocal stab. Played by John "Jabo" Starks. sourceRob Base and DJ E-Z Rock, Cookie Crew
Apache
Incredible Bongo Band - "Apache", 1973		The long drum and bongo break. Played by King Errisson on percussion; kit drummer disputed (Jim Gordon or Kat Hendrikse). sourceSugarhill Gang, Grandmaster Flash, Goldie, Nas
Impeach the President
The Honey Drippers - "Impeach the President", 1973		The opening bar of drums. sourceMC Shan, Audio Two, The Notorious B.I.G., NasWriter-producer Roy C had earned no royalties from sample use as of 2018.
Synthetic Substitution
Melvin Bliss - "Synthetic Substitution", 1973		The kick-heavy opening groove. Played by Bernard Purdie. sourcePublic Enemy, Ultramagnetic MCs, The Pharcyde, Wu-Tang Clan
Levee break
Led Zeppelin - "When the Levee Breaks", 1971		The opening drums, recorded in a stairwell at Headley Grange. Played by John Bonham. sourceBeastie Boys, Dr. Dre, Eminem, BjörkJimmy Page never took legal action over the Beastie Boys' use.
It's a New Day
Skull Snaps - "It's a New Day", 1973		The drums. Played by George Bragg. sourceStezo, The Prodigy, Rob Dougan
The Big Beat
Billy Squier - "The Big Beat", 1980		The opening drums. Played by Bobby Chouinard. sourceRun-DMC, Jay-Z, Dizzee Rascal, Alicia Keys
Mardi Gras
Bob James - "Take Me to the Mardi Gras", 1975		The opening drums and bell pattern. sourceRun-DMC, De La Soul, N.W.A
Nautilus
Bob James - "Nautilus", 1974		The bassline and the sonar-like synth. Played by Gary King (bass). sourceUltramagnetic MC's, Run-DMC, Slick Rick, A Tribe Called Quest
UFO
ESG - "UFO", 1981		The siren-like guitar figure at the top. Played by ESG (the Scroggins sisters). sourceBig Daddy Kane, Public Enemy, The Notorious B.I.G., J DillaESG went about 20 years without royalties from the sampling; payments began after a 2000 compilation.
Ashley's Roachclip
The Soul Searchers - "Ashley's Roachclip", 1974		The break at about 3:36. sourceEric B. and Rakim, PM Dawn, Milli Vanilli, EMF
Papa Was Too
Joe Tex - "Papa Was Too", 1966		Open drums with a tambourine on beat two. sourceEPMD, Wu-Tang Clan, Common, Dr. Dre
Funky President
James Brown - "Funky President (People It's Bad)", 1974		The drums. Played by Allan Schwartzberg. source
The "fresh" scratch
Fab 5 Freddy / Beside - "Change the Beat", 1982		A tone and a vocoder line ending on the word "fresh", at the end of the B-side. Played by Voice disputed: Roger Trilling or Fab 5 Freddy. sourceHerbie Hancock, Eric B. and Rakim, Beastie Boys, MacklemoreWriter Bernard Zekri and Roger Trilling say they were not paid; Beside does not remember whether she signed a contract.
Trans-Europe Express / Numbers
Kraftwerk - "Trans-Europe Express" and "Numbers", 1977 / 1981		The melody of one and the beat of the other, replayed, not sampled, on "Planet Rock". Played by Kraftwerk. sourceAfrika Bambaataa and the Soulsonic Force, CybotronBy the account of those involved, Tommy Boy agreed to pay Kraftwerk $1 per record sold.
Volt Mix
DJ Battery Brain - "8 Volt Mix" (B-side "808 Beatapella Mix"), 1988		A Roland TR-808 beat that anchored 1990s funk carioca. Played by DJ Battery Brain (Mark Rogers). source
Sleng Teng
Wayne Smith - "Under Mi Sleng Teng", 1985		The "rock" rhythm preset of the Casio MT-40. Played by Preset written by Okuda Hiroko; played by Noel Davey. sourceWayne Smith, King JammyCasio chose not to sue and asks for a credit line and no fee.
Thoda Resham Lagta Hai
Lata Mangeshkar - "Thoda Resham Lagta Hai" (film Jyoti), 1981		The vocal recording, used on "Addictive". Played by Composer Bappi Lahiri. sourceTruth Hurts feat. Rakim
More Spell on You
Eddie Johns - "More Spell on You", 1979		The lead sample of "One More Time". Played by Eddie Johns. sourceDaft Punk, Drake and 21 SavageJohns had received nothing as of May 2021; Daft Punk's label says it pays the track's owner.
